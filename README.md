# SnapWindows

SnapWindows puts windows in place on a Mac, the way Windows 11 does. Press
**⌃⌥←** or **⌃⌥→** (Control, Option and an arrow) to put the front window on the
left or right half of the screen, drag a window to an edge of the screen, and
more. This page holds the finished app; SnapWindows checks here once a day for
a new version.

**[Download SnapWindows](https://github.com/beemer2015/SnapWindows-releases/releases/latest/download/SnapWindows.zip)**
for a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## Installing it

You do this once. macOS asks a few times along the way, because SnapWindows is
not registered with Apple.

1. **Download** it with the link above. Safari unpacks it in your Downloads
   folder by itself; with another browser, double-click `SnapWindows.zip`.
2. **Move it first.** In the Finder, drag **SnapWindows** from Downloads into
   **Applications**, before opening it. Opened from Downloads, macOS runs it
   from a temporary copy that can never update.
3. **Open it** from Applications. macOS says *“SnapWindows” Not Opened*, because
   Apple could not check it. Click **Done** (not Move to Trash).
4. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and scroll down to
   **Security**. Next to *“SnapWindows” was blocked to protect your Mac*, click
   **Open Anyway**. The button is there for about an hour after step 3.
5. On *Open “SnapWindows”?*, click **Open Anyway** again and enter your Mac's
   password.

   On macOS 13 Ventura or 14 Sonoma, steps 3 to 5 are simpler: Control-click
   SnapWindows in Applications, choose **Open**, then click **Open**.
6. SnapWindows asks for **Accessibility**, which is what lets it move windows.
   Open System Settings as it shows you and switch **SnapWindows** on. There is
   nothing to restart.

Its icon appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen. Click it for the
menu; **Settings…** shows every shortcut and lets you change them. Holding
⌃⌥ for a moment shows them all too.

## Updates

Once a day SnapWindows looks here for a new version. When there is one, it
does not interrupt you: its menu bar icon gets a small dot, and its menu says
**Update to SnapWindows …**. Choose that to see what is new, click **Install
Update**, then **Install and Relaunch** when it is ready, and SnapWindows
restarts by itself a few seconds later. (Found just after SnapWindows starts,
the window opens by itself.) **Check for Updates…** in the menu looks right
away.

Updates need none of the steps above again. Your settings and the
Accessibility permission stay as they are.

Every version here is signed with a key only its developer has, and
SnapWindows installs nothing that is not signed with it.

## If something goes wrong

- ***“SnapWindows” is damaged and can’t be opened***: the download broke on the
  way. Move it to the Trash and download it again.
- **No Open Anyway button**: open SnapWindows from Applications once more, then
  look again in Privacy & Security.
- ***Move SnapWindows into Applications*** (SnapWindows says this): it was
  opened from Downloads. Click Quit, drag it into Applications, and open it from
  there.
- **Windows do not move**: in System Settings > Privacy & Security >
  Accessibility, SnapWindows must be switched on.
- **An update asks for an administrator's name and password**: your Mac
  account cannot change the Applications folder. Someone with an administrator
  account enters theirs.
